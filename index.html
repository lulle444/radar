How the rug score works

Points add up to a score from 0 to 100. Under 25 is low, 25–49 medium, 50 and up high. It is a quick screen, not a verdict: a low score doesn’t make a token safe.

  • Deployer bag: +30 if the wallet that launched the token still holds over 10%, +15 over 5%.
  • Concentration: +25 if the ten biggest wallets hold over half the supply, +12 over 30%. Pools, bonding curves, lockers and burn addresses don’t count as wallets.
  • Liquidity: +20 under $10k, +10 under $50k, and +10 if liquidity is under 2% of market cap.
  • Activity: +15 if sells outnumber buys 2:1 in the last hour, +15 if the price fell over 50% in an hour, +10 for fewer than 50 holders, +10 in the first hour.

Pools from GeckoTerminal, holders from each chain’s Blockscout explorer. Holders are checked for the 20 deepest new tokens on each refresh. Not financial advice.
